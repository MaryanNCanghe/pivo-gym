Frequently Asked

How do I create a workout?

Go to Workouts → Create workout, name it, pick your days and add exercises from the library.

How do I log meals?

Go to Meals → Search Food or use the camera button to scan a photo of your food.

How do I track my steps?

Go to Steps → Add Steps Manually to log your daily step count.

How do I add my own recipes?

Go to Recipes → My Recipe Book → ＋ Add Recipe and fill in the details.

How do I join a community challenge?

Go to Community → scroll to the challenge board and tap Join on any active challenge.
