Privacy & Security

Your data stays yours — always.

No data selling No third-party ads You own your data Delete anytime

What We Collect

To provide PIVO's features, we collect only what's needed:

  • Account info — your username, email, and password (stored encrypted).
  • Profile data — age, weight, height, gender, and fitness objective you provide voluntarily.
  • Activity logs — meals, steps, workouts, and water you track inside the app.
  • Community content — posts, comments, and images you choose to share publicly.

What We Never Do

  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
  • We never use your data for advertising or share it with ad networks.
  • We never access your health data outside of what you explicitly enter in PIVO.
  • We never share your private profile data (weight, age, etc.) with other users.
  • We never train AI models on your personal data without explicit consent.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to power your personal fitness experience:

  • Show your daily nutrition, macro, and calorie progress.
  • Track your workout history and weekly performance.
  • Display your step count and water intake.
  • Power AI suggestions based on your goals (processed securely, not stored).
  • Show your posts and activity in the Community feed.

How We Protect Your Data

  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard cryptography — never stored in plain text.
  • All connections use HTTPS encryption in transit.
  • Your account is protected by secure session management.
  • Uploaded photos and files are stored in private, access-controlled storage.

Community & Public Content

Posts you share in the Community feed are visible to other PIVO users. Your private stats — weight, macros, goals — are never shown publicly. You can delete your own posts at any time from the community page.

Your Rights

You are in full control of your data:

  • Access — view all data you've entered in your profile and logs.
  • Edit — update your profile, goals, and activity data at any time.
  • Delete — delete your posts, progress photos, and meal logs directly in the app.
  • Account deletion — contact us to permanently delete your account and all associated data.

App Rules & Community Guidelines

By using PIVO, you agree to:

  • Use the app for personal fitness tracking only — not for commercial scraping or abuse.
  • Keep Community posts respectful, supportive, and fitness-related.
  • Not upload offensive, explicit, or harmful content in posts or photos.
  • Not attempt to access other users' private data or circumvent app security.
  • Not use the AI features to generate harmful, misleading, or abusive content.

We reserve the right to remove content or suspend accounts that violate these rules.

Contact

Questions about your privacy or data? Reach out to us at privacy@pivo.fit. We'll respond within 48 hours.
